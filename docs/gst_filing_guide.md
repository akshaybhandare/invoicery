# GST Filing Guide: GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B

This guide outlines the step-by-step process of filing your monthly or quarterly GST returns on the official government portal using the reports and files generated by this application.

---

## 📦 Part 1: How to File GSTR-1 (Sales Outward)

GSTR-1 contains details of all your outward supplies (sales invoices). You will upload the generated JSON file directly to avoid manual entry.

### Step 1: Export the JSON from the App
1. Go to the **Reports** tab.
2. Set the **Period Mode** to *Monthly* or *Quarterly*, and select the target month/quarter.
3. Click the **GSTR-1 (Sales Outward)** sub-tab.
4. Click the **📦 Export Compatible JSON** button. A validated `.json` file will download to your system.

### Step 2: Upload to the GST Portal
1. Log in to the official **[GST Portal](https://www.gst.gov.in/)**.
2. Go to **Services** ➔ **Returns** ➔ **Returns Dashboard**.
3. Select the relevant **Financial Year** and **Filing Period**, then click **Search**.
4. Locate the **Details of outward supplies of goods or services (GSTR-1)** card, and click **Prepare Offline**.
5. Click **Choose File** under the Upload tab, and select the `.json` file you exported in Step 1.
6. The portal will process the file (typically takes 2-5 minutes). 

> [!NOTE]
> If any errors occur during processing, check the error report downloaded from the portal. It highlights issues like missing customer GSTINs or invalid state codes, which you can fix in your customer contacts.

### Step 3: Verify & Submit
1. Go back to the Returns Dashboard and click **Prepare Online** under GSTR-1.
2. Verify that the figures in **Table 4A (B2B)**, **Table 7 (B2CS)**, and **Table 12 (HSN Summary)** match the visual tables shown in the app.
3. Click **Generate Summary**, review the consolidated draft, and click **File Statement** using EVC (OTP sent to your registered phone/email) or DSC.

## 🧮 Part 2: How to File GSTR-3B (Tax Summary & ITC Offset)

GSTR-3B is your monthly self-assessment return where you offset output tax liability with Input Tax Credit (ITC) and pay the remaining balance.

### Step 1: Open the GSTR-3B Summary in the App
1. Under the **Reports** tab, click **GSTR-3B (Tax Summary & ITC)**.
2. Select the target filing period.
3. Keep this screen open; you will copy these exact consolidated values.

### Step 2: Copy Values to the GST Portal
1. Log in to the **[GST Portal](https://www.gst.gov.in/)**, go to the Returns Dashboard, and click **Prepare Online** under **GSTR-3B**.
2. Copy values block-by-block:

#### 1. Outward Supplies (Portal Table 3.1)
- Copy the taxable value, IGST, CGST, and SGST from **Table 3.1** in the app to Table 3.1 on the portal.
- *Section (a)* is for standard taxable B2B/B2C sales.
- *Section (b)* is for zero-rated exports.
- *Section (c)* is for nil-rated/exempted.

#### 2. Eligible Input Tax Credit (Portal Table 4)
- Copy values from **Table 4** in the app.
- **Import of Goods / Services**: If you have imported items.
- **Inward Supplies liable to Reverse Charge (RCM)**: If you purchased from unregistered suppliers or RCM services.
- **All Other ITC**: The main credit compiled from your standard purchases (CGST/SGST/IGST).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Blocked Credits (Section 17(5))**: The application isolates blocked credits (e.g., fuel expenses) from "All Other ITC" and lists them separately. Ensure these are reported under Table 4(D) on the portal as ineligible ITC to keep your filings audit-safe.

### Step 3: Pre-Filing Reconciliation (Compare App vs Portal)
Before completing the filing, perform this verification to ensure your system values and the GST Portal values match perfectly.

1. On the GST Portal, after filling Tables 3.1 and 4, click **Save GSTR-3B** and then **Proceed to Payment**.
2. Scroll to the **Payment of Tax** ledger screen. Locate the **Additional Cash Required** column. This represents your net payable cash liability.
3. Compare these figures with the **GSTR-3B (Tax Summary & ITC)** values in the app:
   - Check the **Net GST Payable** card (CGST, SGST, IGST).
   - Check the **Interest** and **Late Fee** rows.
   - Verify the equation:
     $$\text{App Net GST Payable} + \text{Interest} + \text{Late Fees} = \text{Portal Additional Cash Required}$$
4. If there is a mismatch:
   - **Check GSTR-2B Auto-Population**: The portal's ITC is based on what your suppliers have uploaded. If a supplier hasn't filed their GSTR-1 yet, the portal's ITC will be *lower* than the app's calculated ITC.
   - **Check Blocked Credit Reporting**: Ensure you have listed blocked u/s 17(5) items in Table 4(D) to reconcile the total credit offset.

### Step 4: Pay & File
1. If the numbers match, click **Create Challan** to pay any outstanding cash required.
2. Offset the liabilities and click **File GSTR-3B** using EVC or DSC.
